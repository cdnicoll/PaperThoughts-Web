# Privacy

Paper Thoughts stores your journals in your own iCloud account. We have no servers, no accounts, and no way to read anything you write.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services. Your entries, photos, and locations sync through iCloud using Apple's infrastructure, under Apple's privacy policy and your iCloud settings.

## Features that touch sensitive data

Everything below stays on your device or in your iCloud. None of it reaches us.

- **Location.** If you turn on location tagging, the place you wrote an entry is saved with that entry — nowhere else.
- **State of Mind.** If you log a mood on an entry, it is saved to Apple Health on your device. We never see it.
- **App Lock.** Face ID and Touch ID run entirely on your device through Apple's frameworks. Biometric data never leaves it.

## Purchases

Premium subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through your App Store account. We never see your payment details — Apple reports only anonymous, aggregate sales.

## This website

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking. It is a static page.
